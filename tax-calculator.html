How This Calculator Works

It follows the official IRS method:

  1. Combined Income = Other Income (AGI) + Nontaxable Interest + ½ of Social Security benefits
  2. Compares combined income to the 2026 thresholds
  3. Applies the correct “lesser of” formula for the 50% or 85% tier

For a full explanation, see our Social Security Tax Guide.
