5 Real-World Examples: Documents Required (per SSA POMS)

POMS (Program Operations Manual System) is the official SSA policy manual that governs exactly which documents are acceptable for SSN issuance. The references below (RM 00203 series) detail the evidence rules for identity, citizenship/immigration status, and work authorization. Always bring original unexpired documents only — photocopies are never accepted. Latest official POMS: secure.ssa.gov/poms.nsf

1. U.S. Citizen – Lost or Stolen SSN Card

POMS RM 00203.020 & RM 00203.030 – These sections cover evidence of U.S. citizenship and identity for replacement cards.

  • Valid U.S. driver’s license, state ID, or passport (primary photo ID)
  • Birth certificate or U.S. passport (if no photo ID available)

Why these? POMS requires proof of identity + citizenship. One document can satisfy both if it contains photo + U.S. birth/citizenship info.

2. H-1B Specialty Occupation Visa – First-Time SSN

POMS RM 00203.100 – Evidence of non-citizen immigration status and work authorization for H visas.

  • Valid passport with H-1B visa stamp
  • I-94 arrival record (electronic or paper)
  • Form I-797 approval notice
  • Labor Condition Application (LCA)

Why these? RM 00203.100 specifically lists I-797 + LCA as required for H-1B work-authorized SSN issuance.

3. F-1 Student (OPT/CPT)

POMS RM 00203.100 – Student visa evidence rules requiring school verification of SSN need.

  • Valid passport
  • Form I-20 with DSO SSN endorsement
  • I-94 record
  • EAD card (for OPT)
  • School letter confirming SSN is required for employment or banking

Why these? POMS mandates the I-20 endorsement + school letter to confirm the SSN is needed for authorized F-1 activity.

4. Green Card / Permanent Resident

POMS RM 00203.100 – Lawful permanent resident (LPR) evidence requirements.

  • Green Card (Form I-551)
  • Valid passport

Why these? RM 00203.100 accepts the I-551 Green Card as primary proof of immigration status and work authorization.

5. EAD / Employment Authorization Holder

POMS RM 00203.100 – Employment Authorization Document rules for non-immigrant categories.

  • Valid passport
  • Form I-766 EAD card
  • I-797 (if the EAD was based on a pending application)

Why these? POMS RM 00203.100 treats the I-766 EAD as definitive proof of work authorization for SSN issuance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I replace my SSN card online?
Yes — eligible U.S. citizens (18+, no record changes) can replace instantly via my Social Security.
What documents do I need as a non-citizen?
Original passport + I-94 + visa/EAD/I-797/I-20/DS-2019. Photocopies are not accepted.
How long does it take?
Usually 7–14 business days by mail. No expedited service.
First-time applicant?
Same in-person process — bring job offer or school letter if needed.
