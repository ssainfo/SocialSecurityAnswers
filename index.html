5 Real-World Examples: Documents Required (per SSA POMS)

POMS (Program Operations Manual System) is the official SSA policy manual. Always bring original unexpired documents only.

1. U.S. Citizen – Lost or Stolen SSN Card

POMS RM 00203.020 & RM 00203.030

  • Valid U.S. driver’s license, state ID, or passport
  • Birth certificate or U.S. passport

2. H-1B Specialty Occupation Visa – First-Time SSN

POMS RM 00203.100

  • Valid passport with H-1B visa stamp
  • I-94 arrival record
  • Form I-797 approval notice
  • Labor Condition Application (LCA)

3. F-1 Student (OPT/CPT)

POMS RM 00203.100

  • Valid passport
  • Form I-20 with DSO SSN endorsement
  • I-94 record
  • EAD card (for OPT)
  • School letter

4. Green Card / Permanent Resident

POMS RM 00203.100

  • Green Card (Form I-551)
  • Valid passport

5. EAD / Employment Authorization Holder

POMS RM 00203.100

  • Valid passport
  • Form I-766 EAD card
  • I-797 (if applicable)

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I replace my SSN card online?
Yes — eligible U.S. citizens (18+, no record changes) can replace instantly via my Social Security.
What documents do I need as a non-citizen?
Original passport + I-94 + visa/EAD/I-797/I-20/DS-2019. Photocopies are not accepted.
How long does it take?
Usually 7–14 business days by mail. No expedited service.
First-time applicant?
Same in-person process — bring job offer or school letter if needed.
