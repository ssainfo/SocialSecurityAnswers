Social Security Card Replacement or First-Time SSN Application 2026

Lost, stolen, or damaged card? Need a brand-new SSN as an immigrant or visa holder? Both processes are free. This guide covers citizens and all major visas.

✓ First-Time SSN Application (New Immigrants & Visa Holders)

Most new arrivals must apply in person after 10 days in the U.S. Free • Usually 7–14 business days by mail.

Wait 10 Days After Entry

Allow DHS/SEVIS records to update.

Gather Original Documents

Passport + I-94 + I-797 (or I-20 / DS-2019 / EAD) + job or school letter when required.

Start Online & Schedule Appointment

Go to ssa.gov — the system will direct you to an ICON appointment when needed.

Attend Appointment

Bring all original documents. SSA can issue the SSN the same day if needed for work or school.

Receive Card

Mailed to your U.S. address, typically within 7–14 business days.

Pro tip: Always use original unexpired documents. Check your I-94 at i94.cbp.dhs.gov before your appointment.

Replacement Process for Existing SSN Holders

Limits: 3 per year • 10 lifetime (exceptions for name or work-authorization changes). Processing is usually about 10 business days.

Check Eligibility

U.S. citizens age 18+ with no record changes can often replace online.

Apply

Use my Social Security or submit Form SS-5 with required documents.

Receive Card

Card is mailed. Call 1-800-772-1213 if it has not arrived after 3 weeks.

5 Real-World Document Examples (per SSA POMS)

Always bring original unexpired documents. Photocopies are not accepted. Official policy is in the SSA Program Operations Manual System (POMS) RM 00203 series.

1. U.S. Citizen – Lost or Stolen Card

POMS RM 00203.020 / .030

  • Valid U.S. driver’s license, state ID, or passport
  • Birth certificate (if no photo ID is available)

One document can sometimes satisfy both identity and citizenship.

2. H-1B Visa – First-Time SSN

POMS RM 00203.100

  • Passport with H-1B visa stamp
  • I-94 arrival record
  • Form I-797 approval notice
  • Labor Condition Application (LCA)

3. F-1 Student (OPT / CPT)

POMS RM 00203.100

  • Passport + I-20 with DSO endorsement
  • I-94 record
  • EAD card (for OPT)
  • School letter confirming SSN is needed

4. Green Card / Permanent Resident

POMS RM 00203.100

  • Green Card (Form I-551)
  • Valid passport

5. EAD / Work Authorization

POMS RM 00203.100

  • Passport
  • Form I-766 EAD card
  • I-797 (if the EAD was based on a pending application)

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I replace my SSN card online?

Yes. Eligible U.S. citizens age 18+ with no name or other record changes can usually replace their card online through my Social Security.

What documents do non-citizens need?

Original passport + I-94 + visa / EAD / I-797 / I-20 / DS-2019. Photocopies are never accepted.

How long does it take?

Usually 7–14 business days by mail. There is no official expedited service.

First-time applicant?

Most first-time applicants (especially non-citizens) must apply in person. Bring a job offer or school letter when required.
